# Local and seasonal

Something is happening where the user's customers are: the first freeze, a hail storm, a filing deadline, the start of a school term. Say the one useful thing about it.

## Timing and care

- Draft it when the event is near or just happened. A week late is too late: tell the user instead.
- After damage or a hard event, lead with what helps people. Never sound pleased about it, and never offer a "storm special".
- Only refer to an event the user told you about or that you can see in their own leads and replies. Do not guess the weather.

## Shape

1. What is happening, and where.
2. The one thing to check or do this week.
3. What the user can do about it, in one line.

## By platform

- **Facebook:** 40 to 90 words. Name the town or area.
- **Instagram:** two or three lines and a photo the user took.
- **LinkedIn:** 60 to 120 words, for the owners and managers who have to plan for it.

## Do not

- Create urgency that is not real
- Mention prices or discounts
- Post about a tragedy at all unless the user asks, in so many words
