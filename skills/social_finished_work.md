# Finished work

Show a job that is done. Proof beats claims: one real job says more than any line about quality.

## What you need from the user before drafting

- Which job, where (the neighbourhood or town, never the street address), and what was wrong
- One detail that made it hard or interesting
- Photos, and that the customer is happy for them to be shown

Never invent a job, a number or a customer. If you do not have a real one, ask for one and draft nothing.

## Shape

1. The place and the problem, in one line.
2. What was done, with the one detail.
3. How long it took or what changed for the customer.
4. One line for the next person with the same problem. No hard sell.

## By platform

- **LinkedIn:** 60 to 120 words. Lead with the problem. Speak to the people who hire you (owners, managers), not to the public at large.
- **Facebook:** 40 to 80 words, plainer and warmer. Name the town.
- **Instagram:** the photos carry it. Two or three short lines, then at most five tags that a local would search for.

## Media note

Say which photos go with the post and in what order (before first), in the media note. Sellbird stores no images.

## Do not

- Name the customer or show a house number or a face without their say-so
- Say "best", "top rated" or "number one"
- Use more than one exclamation mark, or any emoji the user would not type themselves
