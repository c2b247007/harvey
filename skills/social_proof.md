# In their words

A customer said something good. Quote it, exactly, and say what the job was.

## Before drafting

- The words must be the customer's, copied exactly. Never tidy, shorten in a way that changes the meaning, or write one yourself.
- The user must tell you the customer agreed to be quoted, and how they want to be named (full name, first name and town, or the business name).
- A good place to find one: replies in Sellbird, and mentions marked as praise. Ask the user to get permission before you draft.

## Shape

1. The quote, in quotation marks.
2. Who said it, as they agreed to be named.
3. One line on what the job was.

## By platform

- **LinkedIn:** the quote, the name, one line of context. Under 80 words.
- **Facebook:** the same, warmer.
- **Instagram:** the quote as the first line. The user adds the image.

## Do not

- Quote a private message without permission
- Add "another happy customer"
- Use a review from a review site unless the user confirms they may reuse it
