# Customer questions

Take one question the user hears all the time and answer it properly. The reader should be able to act on the answer without ever contacting the user. That is what makes them remember who told them.

## Where the question comes from

In this order: the user's own replies and leads in Sellbird (what people actually asked), the questions on their mentions, then what the user tells you. Do not make up a question nobody asked.

## Shape

1. The question, in the customer's words.
2. The short answer first.
3. The one thing people get wrong, and what to check.
4. When they do need a professional. One line.

## By platform

- **LinkedIn:** 80 to 150 words. One idea. Short lines.
- **Facebook:** 50 to 100 words.
- **Instagram:** the short answer and the one check, in three or four lines.
- **X:** the short answer alone, under 280 characters. No thread unless the user asks.

## Do not

- Give legal, medical, financial or safety advice beyond what the user's trade covers
- State a price, a regulation or a statistic unless the user gave it to you
- End with "DM us" on every post. Once in three is plenty
